Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. About RuneRadar

RuneRadar is an independent RuneLite plugin that provides Grand Exchange recommendations, flip tracking, profit tracking and related market tools for Old School RuneScape players.


2. Information sent to RuneRadar servers

RuneRadar communicates with a RuneRadar-operated backend service in order to provide recommendations and market data.

Depending on the feature being used, the plugin may send:

  • Your configured RuneRadar cash stack.
  • Your RuneRadar recommendation settings.
  • A randomly generated anonymous installation ID.
  • The installed RuneRadar plugin version.
  • Technical request information needed to provide the service.

3. Anonymous installation ID

RuneRadar generates a random installation ID so that basic anonymous usage statistics can be measured.

The installation ID is not based on your RuneScape username, account ID, password or login credentials.


4. Usage analytics

RuneRadar may record basic usage information such as:

  • The first and most recent time an installation used the service.
  • The RuneRadar plugin version.
  • The Free or Pro plan associated with an installation.
  • The number and type of RuneRadar API requests made.

This information is used to understand RuneRadar usage, maintain the service and improve future versions.


5. RuneScape account information

RuneRadar does not intentionally use your RuneScape username, account ID or login credentials as your RuneRadar analytics identity.

RuneRadar never needs your RuneScape password.


6. Server and hosting logs

Infrastructure and hosting providers may process normal technical information such as IP addresses, timestamps and request metadata as part of operating, securing and troubleshooting web services.

RuneRadar does not use an IP address as the installation identifier for its own usage analytics.


7. Payments

RuneRadar Pro is not currently available for purchase.

If paid subscriptions are introduced, payment details will be handled by a third-party payment provider such as Stripe. RuneRadar will not store full card or bank account details on its own servers.

This Privacy Policy will be updated before paid subscriptions become generally available.


8. Data retention

RuneRadar may retain anonymous installation analytics and service records for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, secure and improve the service.


9. Third-party services

RuneRadar depends on third-party infrastructure and services, including RuneLite, GitHub and hosting providers.

These third parties may have their own privacy policies and terms.


10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when RuneRadar changes, including when accounts, subscriptions or additional online services are introduced.


11. Contact

For privacy questions or issues, contact RuneRadar through the project's GitHub repository.
